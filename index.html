Who I Am

Hi, I'm Tarek Mahmud Jihad — a passionate Penetration Tester and Bug Hunter from Noakhali, Bangladesh. I specialize in uncovering vulnerabilities in web applications before malicious actors can exploit them.

My journey into cybersecurity started with curiosity about how systems could be broken and, more importantly, how they could be fixed. Since then, I've been deeply involved in ethical hacking, bug bounty platforms, and security research.
